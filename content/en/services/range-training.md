The range is better with company. A second set of eyes, a spotter for your
groups, and someone who knows the safety rules as well as you do.

I'm not an instructor and I don't hand out advice you didn't ask for. What I
offer is a safe, familiar presence: someone to go with, to watch the line while
you shoot, to confirm a zero, and to make the trip a little more enjoyable than
going alone.

## What I can help with

- Going along as a range companion and extra set of eyes
- Spotting for groups and confirming your zero
- Watching the line and helping keep things safe
- Loading magazines, setting up targets, and packing up
- Basic maintenance and cleaning of your firearms

## How it works

Tell me when and where you're planning to go, and what you're hoping to get out
of the trip. We'll meet at the range. I bring my own gear and eye and ear
protection, and I follow the range's rules and yours.

Safety comes first, always — if something looks off on the line, I'll say so.
Beyond that, we go at your pace and enjoy the day.
