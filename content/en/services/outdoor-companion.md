More hands makes less work — and most jobs outdoors go better with someone
along.

Whether it's clearing a yard, splitting and stacking firewood, hauling brush,
moving furniture, or fixing something that finally gave out, I'm happy to lend
a hand and a strong back. I come with my own tools, I show up when I say I
will, and I don't need to be managed while we work.

If you'd rather not tackle something alone — or you just want company while you
do it — that's exactly the sort of thing I'm glad to help with.

## What I can help with

- Yard and property cleanup, brush hauling, and storm debris
- Splitting, stacking, and moving firewood
- Lifting, carrying, and moving heavy or awkward things
- Small repairs, assembly, and odd jobs around the place
- Keeping you company on a task that's easier with two people

## How it works

Reach out with what you're trying to get done, when works for you, and roughly
where you are. We'll agree on a time, and I'll bring the tools the job calls
for. For small jobs a handshake is plenty; for bigger ones we can sort out the
details up front so there are no surprises.
