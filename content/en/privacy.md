# Privacy

The short version: I collect nothing about you on purpose. Anything that is
collected here is incidental — a by-product of a thing you chose to do.

---

## What that means

If you place an order, send a message, make a payment, or otherwise interact
with this site, the details you provide are used to handle that interaction and
nothing else. There is no analytics tracker, no advertising pixel, no profile
being built about you.

## Third parties

Some parts of ordering, messaging, and paying run through services I do not
control — payment processors such as Stripe, delivery and fulfilment services,
mail handlers, and the like. Those services may receive a small amount of data
as a necessary part of doing their job, and that is unavoidable if you want the
thing to work. Each of them handles that data under its own terms, not mine.

## No promises

I make no promises here. This page describes how I intend to operate, not a
binding guarantee, and it can change at any time without notice. If that is not
good enough for what you are about to do, please do not do it.
